**Zupermarket Zimulator 0.14.0: ray tracing. Every light casts real shadows, in the normal view and in first person.**

## Downloads
- **Android** (phones, tablets, AYN Thor and other handhelds): `ZupermarketZimulator-android.apk`
- **Windows** (64-bit): `ZupermarketZimulator-windows.exe` (a single file, no installer)

It installs over earlier versions and keeps your saves. On Windows, 0.9.3 and later update themselves to this version.

## New in 0.14.0
- **Ray tracing:** a new level in **Options > Graphics**, after High.
  - Every light casts shadows: the ceiling lights, street lamps, burning barrels, the cauldron, lanterns, the shops' counter lights and the OPEN sign.
  - Shelves, coolers, racks, walls, counters, boxes, wrecked cars and ruins cast shadows. So do you, the shoppers, your staff and the skeletons.
  - The shadows have soft edges, and the side of a shelf facing away from the lights is darker.
  - Walls and roofs keep the street lamps out of the stores at night.
  - It works in first person too: the floor, walls and shelves are lit, shadows and all.
- **On by default on PC.** If you had High, you get Ray tracing now (pick High again in Options to go back).
- **Phones start on Medium.** Switch to Ray tracing in Options on a fast phone or handheld like the AYN Thor.
- It runs on any graphics card that runs the game. There's no RTX card needed: the rays are traced in the game's own shaders.

## Installing
- **Android / AYN Thor:** open the APK link in the device's browser and tap the download. Android will ask you to allow installs from that browser (Settings → Install unknown apps). Allow it, go back and tap **Install**.
- **Windows:** on 0.9.3 or later, the game updates itself. Otherwise download it once. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway** (the game isn't code-signed).
