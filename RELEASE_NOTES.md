**Zupermarket Zimulator 0.17.0: two new apps on the store computer. Snake, and ZomTok (the town's characters dancing, with likes and comments).**

## Downloads
- **Android** (phones, tablets, AYN Thor and other handhelds): `ZupermarketZimulator-android.apk`
- **Windows** (64-bit): `ZupermarketZimulator-windows.exe` (a single file, no installer)

It installs over earlier versions and keeps your saves. On Windows, 0.9.3 and later update themselves to this version.

## New in 0.17.0
- **Snake** (Zombie Snake), on the store computer:
  - Steer a snake round the screen eating what your store sells. Every bite makes it longer and a little faster; hit a wall or yourself and it's over.
  - Steer with the arrows or WASD, the d-pad or stick, a swipe, or the on-screen arrows.
  - Your best score is saved with your store, and it pauses when you leave the app.
- **ZomTok**, on the store computer: videos of the town dancing, one at a time on a phone screen, each with its own beat.
  - **Who:** zombies, skeletons, witches, vampires and the other shoppers you've met, your own staff (on the clock), Mr. Bones advertising Bone Mart, Count Vlad, and Big Rotto's crew once the gang war's on.
  - **Where:** your store, Bone Mart, the street, the graveyard or a crypt party.
  - **Next video:** swipe up (or Down, the mouse wheel, the d-pad).
  - **Like it** with the heart, a double tap, Space or A. You can also follow whoever posted it, or share it.
  - **Comments:** the bubble (C, or X on a controller). Type your own or pick a quick reply, and the poster might like it.
  - A new feed every day.

## Installing
- **Android / AYN Thor:** open the APK link in the device's browser and tap the download. Android will ask you to allow installs from that browser (Settings → Install unknown apps). Allow it, go back and tap **Install**.
- **Windows:** on 0.9.3 or later, the game updates itself. Otherwise download it once. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway** (the game isn't code-signed).
